## Recommendation

Recommended: A · Ledger Green

PRODUCT.md describes frequent, short, one-handed entries, so the open row treatment gives the product choice and log action clear priority without adding steps. The restrained green separates actions from confirmed figures while preserving the ink-on-paper precision across the ledger and descriptive Insights. Its clear grotesk and mono pairing supports both quick scanning and tabular values while keeping the interface discreet.
